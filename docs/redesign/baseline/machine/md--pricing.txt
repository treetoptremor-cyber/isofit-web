# Logging is free. Pro is $14.99 a month.

Source: https://isofit.app/pricing
Facts last reviewed: 2026-09-23

Isofit has two tiers. Free includes unlimited workout logging, Quicklog, routines, full history, Apple Health import, data export and 10 Atlas messages a month. Pro is planned at $14.99 a month or $124.99 a year in the US App Store, and adds the full body graph, higher Atlas limits with deep analysis and saved programs, SITREP (weekly training review), and posting to Bonfire. Isofit is not released yet, so these prices are planned and the App Store listing will be final.

## How billing works

- **Where you pay:** Pro is an in-app subscription bought through the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment; Isofit never sees your card.
- **Plans:** Monthly at $14.99 or yearly at $124.99. The yearly plan works out to about $10.42 a month.
- **Currency:** USD, US App Store. Prices vary by region and the App Store listing is final.
- **Voice logging:** Voice logging powered by earned $ISO. Each successfully processed voice entry costs 0.05 $ISO on all plans and requires sufficient balance: with no $ISO the voice log is blocked, and a failed entry is refunded. Usage limits apply.
- **Cancelling:** Cancel any time in your Apple account settings. Cancelling does not delete your account or your training data.
- **Deleting your account:** Deleting your Isofit account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel the subscription with Apple as well, or Apple keeps billing you.
- **$ISO and cancelling:** Cancelling Pro forfeits your $ISO balance and resets your streak. $ISO has no cash value.

## What $ISO is

$ISO is Isofit's in-app points. You earn it by logging workouts and spend it on small actions inside the app. It is not money: it has no cash value and exists only inside Isofit.

These are the rates at launch. They are set on Isofit's servers and can change. Backdated sessions never earn $ISO, and every member gets one streak freeze a month, applied automatically before a streak breaks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Isofit cost?

Isofit is free to log with. Pro is planned at $14.99 a month or $124.99 a year in the US App Store. Prices vary by region and the App Store listing is final.

### What do I get for free in Isofit?

Unlimited workout logging by tap and Quicklog, voice logging for 0.05 earned $ISO an entry, unlimited routines, full session history, the exercise library and custom exercises, Apple Health import, data export, and 10 Atlas messages a month. In Bonfire you can read, give kudos and comment.

### What does Isofit Pro add?

The full body graph with muscle detail and stats, 30 Atlas messages a day and 100 deep-analysis requests a month, Atlas programs saved to your routines, SITREP (weekly training review), posting to Bonfire, and higher $ISO earning.

### Is there a limit on workouts or routines on the Free tier?

No. Workouts, routines and history are unlimited on Free.

### Does Apple Health sync cost extra?

No. Connecting Apple Health is optional and included on every tier.

### If I cancel Pro, do I lose my workout data?

No. Cancelling moves you to Free and keeps your account, log and history. You do forfeit your $ISO balance and your streak resets.

### Can I buy Isofit today?

Not yet. Coming soon to iPhone and the web. Joining the waitlist is free and carries no obligation to subscribe.
