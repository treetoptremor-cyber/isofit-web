# Isofit vs Hevy: which workout app fits how you train?

Source: https://isofit.app/compare/isofit-vs-hevy
Facts last reviewed: 2026-09-23

Hevy is a free workout tracker for iOS and Android built around reusable routines, structured set logging and a social feed of friends' workouts. Isofit is a workout tracker that takes workouts by tap, typed line or voice, shows the muscles your sets reached on a body graph, and includes Atlas, an AI coach that can read your log. Hevy is released and established; Isofit is pre-release. Coming soon to iPhone and the web. The short version: pick Hevy if you train with friends who already use Hevy and want a shared feed and leaderboards; pick Isofit if you want plain-language logging and a coach built into the app.

## Choose Hevy if

- You train with friends who already use Hevy and want a shared feed and leaderboards.
- You need Android, Apple Watch, Wear OS or a web app today.
- You want demonstration animations for every library exercise.
- You want a low-cost or lifetime plan: Hevy lists Pro at $2.99 a month, $23.99 a year or $74.99 once.
- You already use ChatGPT and want to chat about your training there, with your Hevy history connected.

## Choose Isofit if

- You want to log in plain language, typed or spoken, and not only by tapping in sets.
- You want a coach built into the app you log with, one that reads your own training history and stays within training.
- You train across disciplines, such as lifting plus running, yoga or martial arts, and want one log for all of it.
- You use an iPhone and do not need a watch, tablet or Android app.
- You want Apple Health read but never written to.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Isofit a good alternative to Hevy?

It depends on what you want from a log. Isofit fits if you want to log in plain language, see sets per muscle on a body graph, and ask an AI coach about your own history, all on iPhone. Hevy fits better if you train with friends who already use Hevy and want a shared feed and leaderboards.

### Is Isofit available now, like Hevy?

No. Hevy is available today. Isofit is pre-release. Coming soon to iPhone and the web.
