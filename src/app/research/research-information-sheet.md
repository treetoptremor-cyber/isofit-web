# Isofit Research Information Sheet

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** August 9, 2026
**Researcher:** Isofit ltd., conducted by its founder
**Contact:** research@isofit.app

---

This sheet explains the research you are invited to join when you tick **Participate in research** in the Isofit app, so you can decide with full information. Participation is optional, you must be 18 or older, and saying no changes nothing about how Isofit works for you.

---

## 1. Who Is Doing This Research and Why

Isofit is built by a solo developer who conducts academic research on how people use AI-assisted fitness tools: how they log training, how they use an AI coach like Atlas, what helps them stay consistent, and how coaching advice relates to what people actually do in the gym. The aim is to publish what we learn so that better tools can be built, by us and by others.

The work is currently conducted independently by Isofit's founder. In the future it may be done together with university partners; Section 7 explains what that would mean for your data.

---

## 2. What Participating Involves

Nothing extra. You use Isofit exactly as you normally would. If you opt in, de-identified data from your account may be included in our analyses. There are no surveys, visits, or tasks.

If we ever want to ask more of you, such as a short survey or an interview, we will ask separately and you can decline without affecting your participation or your account.

---

## 3. What Data Is Included

- **Workout logs:** Exercises, sets, reps, weight, RPE, session notes, and quick logs, together with when you trained.
- **App usage:** Which features you use and when, such as logging, Atlas, streaks, and rewards, and how long you spend in the app.
- **Atlas interactions:** The text of your conversations with Atlas, including transcribed voice logs, and Atlas's responses. Photos and videos you submit for form checks are **not** included.
- **Program settings:** The choices that shape your program, such as age range, goals, experience level, and membership tier.

**Not included:** your name, email address, phone number, username, display name, avatar, bio, Bonfire nickname, Bonfire posts and comments, payment information, and Apple Health or connected-platform data unless a specific study asks for it and you agree to that separately.

---

## 4. How We Protect Your Identity

- Before any analysis, we remove the identifying details listed above and replace your account with a random research code.
- Free text you typed to Atlas is screened for names, places, employers, and other details that could identify you before it is used.
- Results are reported in aggregate. We never attempt to re-identify anyone, and we do not report on groups so small that an individual could be picked out.
- Access to research data is limited to the founder and, under the conditions in Section 7, partner researchers.

---

## 5. Risks and Benefits

**Risks.** This research is minimal risk. Participation does not change your coaching, your program, or anything else in the app. The main risk is to privacy: there is a small chance that de-identified data could be linked back to a person if it were combined with other information. The protections in Section 4 exist to make that as unlikely as we can.

**Benefits.** There is no direct benefit to you beyond the thank-you described below. Your participation helps us understand how AI coaching works for real people, and we will publish plain-language summaries of what we learn on this page.

---

## 6. The Thank-You

When you opt in, we add 5 $ISO to your balance once. It is a thank-you, not a payment for your data: it does not depend on how much you use the app, it is yours to keep even if you withdraw later, and it is not added again if you opt out and back in.

---

## 7. Who Sees the Data

- **The founder** conducts the analysis. Where an AI service is used to help analyze de-identified text, it is used under terms that prohibit the provider from training on the data.
- **Supabase** hosts all Isofit account data, research or not, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). **OpenAI** processes your Atlas conversations to generate coaching responses; that is part of using Atlas, not part of this research.
- **University partners**, if a study involves one, receive only de-identified data, under a written data-sharing agreement, and only after that institution's ethics review of the study. We will update this sheet when a partner joins.

We do not sell research data, and we never use it for advertising.

---

## 8. Publication

Findings may appear in academic papers, conference presentations, theses, and Isofit's own writing. They are reported in aggregate. Short excerpts of Atlas conversations may be quoted as examples, only after anything that could identify you has been removed or paraphrased. Published results cannot be tied back to you.

---

## 9. Duration and Retention

This is an ongoing research program with no fixed end date; individual studies typically look at weeks to months of app use. Your data may be included from the moment you opt in until you withdraw. De-identified research datasets are kept for as long as needed to support published findings, and then deleted. Your account data itself follows the retention rules in our Privacy Policy.

---

## 10. Withdrawing

Open **Settings**, then **Privacy & Terms**, and turn off **Participate in research**. Withdrawal takes effect immediately for the future: your data is no longer included in new analyses. Data already included in de-identified analyses or publications cannot be removed, because it can no longer be traced to you. Withdrawing changes nothing else about your account, and you keep the thank-you. Deleting your account also ends your participation.

---

## 11. Ethics Review

This research is currently conducted independently and has not been reviewed by an institutional review board or independent ethics committee. Any study conducted with a university partner will go through that institution's ethics review before any data is shared, and we will update this sheet if the review status changes.

---

## 12. Questions and Your Rights

- **About the research:** research@isofit.app
- **About your data or your privacy rights**, including access and deletion: privacy@isofit.app, and Section 13 of our [Privacy Policy](/privacy)

If you are in the EU or EEA, the legal basis for including your data in research is your consent, which you may withdraw at any time as described above, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

---

## 13. Changes to This Sheet

We will update this sheet and its effective date when the research changes. If we change what data is included or who can see it in a way that matters, we will update the in-app consent as well and tell participants by email, and you can withdraw at any time.
